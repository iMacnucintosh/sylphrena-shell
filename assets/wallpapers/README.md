# Wallpapers that ship with the shell

These images are **not** covered by the GPL of the shell: each keeps its own licence.

## `trail-of-footprints.jpg`

"A Trail of Footprints In The Sand" by **David Emrich**, <https://unsplash.com/photos/A9mr3TPoj0k>
(Unsplash licence: <https://unsplash.com/license>). Credited in the `debian/copyright` of
<https://github.com/elementary/wallpapers> (branch `deb-packaging`), where it was taken from.

Full resolution (5299 × 5301 px), recompressed as JPEG quality 90 without chroma subsampling (the original was
quality 100, 12.8 MB): 6.2 MB, no visible difference (PSNR ≈ 51 dB).

It is the wallpaper of the first activity on a new install: `scripts/setup-defaults.py` points Principal at it the
first time Sylphrena runs.
